Redmond Lab

David Redmond, PhD · Assistant Professor of Computational Biology, Weill Cornell

Blood vessels are not one tissue. A liver sinusoid, a brain capillary and a kidney glomerulus share an endothelial lineage and little else — every organ writes its own vascular identity, and most of that instruction is still unread.

We build the reference frameworks that make it legible: cross-organ atlases of endothelial and immune cell state, and the transcription factor codes underneath them. Then we ask the harder question — whether that identity can be rewritten. We develop the methods we need to do it, from single-cell and spatial analysis through to deep learning on tissue images.
